How these rates are calculated

Pakistan does not set its gold price in isolation. The local rate is the international spot price of gold, converted into rupees at the live dollar rate, and then adjusted for weight and purity. The dollar rate itself is set by the interbank market, where banks trade currencies with each other all day.

Gold per tola

One tola is 11.6638 grams — about 0.375 of a troy ounce, the unit gold is traded in worldwide. We take the live spot price per troy ounce in US dollars, multiply by the live USD/PKR rate, and multiply by 0.375. That gives the 24-karat price per tola. 22K and 21K prices are the same figure scaled by purity: 22/24 and 21/24.

Dollar to rupee

The USD/PKR rate moves with supply and demand: exports, imports, remittances from Pakistanis abroad, foreign investment and the State Bank's reserves all push it in one direction or the other. The rate on this page is the interbank rate — the rate banks use with each other. The rate a money changer offers you for cash will be close, but not identical.
