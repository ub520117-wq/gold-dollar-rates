About the Saudi Riyal to rupee rate

The Saudi riyal is pegged to the US dollar at a fixed rate of 3.75 riyals per dollar, so — like the UAE dirham — it moves against the rupee in step with the dollar. Saudi Arabia hosts the largest Pakistani expatriate community of any country, and remittances from the Kingdom are the single largest source of foreign currency flowing into Pakistan.

The riyal's peg means the SAR/PKR rate is the dollar rate divided by 3.75. There is no separate riyal story to follow: watch the dollar, and you are watching the riyal. The practical differences are in transfer costs — banks, exchange companies and apps all take different spreads, and the cheapest route for a large amount is not always the cheapest for a small one.

Beyond remittances, the riyal rate matters to the millions of Pakistanis who travel to Saudi Arabia for Umrah and Hajj each year. Pilgrims pay for hotels, transport and expenses in riyals, so the rate decides the real cost of the journey in rupees.
