About the UAE Dirham to rupee rate

The UAE dirham is pegged to the US dollar at a fixed rate of 3.6725 dirhams per dollar, so the dirham moves against the rupee almost exactly in step with the dollar. The UAE is home to one of the largest Pakistani expatriate communities in the world, and Dubai and Abu Dhabi are among the biggest sources of remittances to Pakistan.

Because of the dollar peg, the dirham-to-rupee rate is essentially the dollar rate divided by 3.6725. That makes it unusually predictable: if you know the dollar rate, you know the dirham rate. What varies is what money changers and transfer services actually offer — their spreads differ, so comparing two or three options before sending money home is always worth a few minutes.

The UAE is also Pakistan's largest trading partner in the Gulf and a transit hub for goods, so the dirham rate matters to importers and exporters as well as to families receiving remittances.
