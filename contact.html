Contact

Spotted a wrong number, or want a currency or calculator we do not have yet? Send a message — the button opens your own email app with everything filled in, so nothing is stored on this site.

What we can help with

Corrections to a rate or a calculation, requests for new currencies or tools, and questions about how the rates are worked out. Every message is read, and corrections are checked the same day where possible.

Please note: we cannot give personal financial advice, recommend a jeweller or money changer, or intervene with a bank. For those, a qualified adviser in your own country is the right contact.

Before you write

Rates not loading? The rates come from live data feeds. If a page shows no rates, try the refresh button on the page, then try again in a few minutes — the feeds occasionally pause, and the page says so rather than showing a stale number.

Rate differs from your jeweller? That is expected: this site shows the metal value from the international spot price, while jewellers add making charges and shop premiums. The gold rate page explains the difference in detail.

Want a currency added? Tell us which one and why — if the data feed carries it, we will add the page.
