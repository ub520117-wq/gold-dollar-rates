Copyright & DMCA

How to report copyright infringement on this site.

Last updated: 14 November 2026. All text on this website is original content written for this site. Market data (gold and currency rates) consists of factual figures from third-party feeds and is not copyrightable expression.

Reporting copyright infringement

If you believe material on this site infringes your copyright, please send a notice to the contact email on our contact page with: (1) identification of the copyrighted work; (2) identification of the material on this site you claim is infringing, including its URL; (3) your name, address and email; (4) a statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorised; and (5) a statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorised to act on the owner's behalf.

Our response

We will review valid notices promptly and remove or disable access to material found to be infringing. We may also notify the party responsible for the material and, where appropriate, restore material following a valid counter-notice.

Repeat infringers

We do not host user-generated content. Any third-party content found to infringe will be removed, and we will not knowingly republish it.
