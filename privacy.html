Privacy Policy

What this site collects (almost nothing) and how advertising cookies work.

Last updated: 14 November 2026. This Privacy Policy explains what information this website does and does not collect, how cookies are used, and the choices you have.

Converters and your data

All converters and calculators on this site run entirely in your own browser. The figures you enter are never transmitted to us or to any third party, are never stored, and disappear when you close the page. We have no user accounts and no registration.

Live rate data

To show live rates, your browser fetches data directly from third-party rate providers (named on each rate page). Those requests are made by your browser to the providers' servers; we do not see or store them. The providers may log standard request information such as IP address, as described in their own privacy policies.

Advertising and cookies

This site may display advertising served by third-party advertising networks, which may include Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info/choices. For more about how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see Google's advertising policies.

Third-party ad servers or ad networks may use technologies such as cookies, JavaScript or web beacons in the advertisements that appear on this site, which are sent directly to your browser. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns and/or to personalise the advertising content you see. We have no access to or control over cookies used by third-party advertisers.

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where required by law, advertising and analytics cookies are used only with your consent, collected through a certified consent management platform when ads are active. You can withdraw consent at any time through the same consent controls.

Log files

Like most websites, our hosting may keep standard server log files (IP address, browser type, referring page, date and time of visit) for security and operational purposes. These are not linked to any personally identifiable information.

Children's privacy

This site is a general-audience rates and education site and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the "last updated" date above will be revised.
