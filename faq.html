Frequently asked questions

The questions readers ask us most about gold rates, the dollar rate, and how this site works.

What is the gold rate in Pakistan today?

The live rate is shown at the top of the home page and on the gold rate page. It is calculated from the international spot price of gold and the live US dollar to Pakistani rupee rate, and it updates every time you load the page. Because both inputs move during the day, the rate you see in the morning can differ from the rate in the evening.

How is the per-tola gold price calculated?

Per-tola 24K gold in rupees equals the spot price per troy ounce in US dollars, multiplied by the USD/PKR rate, multiplied by 0.375 (one tola is 0.375 of a troy ounce). 22K gold is that figure times 22/24, and 21K gold is that figure times 21/24. The full formula with a worked example is in our guide on how the gold rate is calculated.

Why is the jeweller's price higher than the rate on this site?

This site shows the value of the metal itself. A jeweller adds making charges for turning gold into jewellery, a shop premium, and sometimes a margin for the day's price movement. Making charges vary by design and are not refunded when you sell the piece back. The board rate for plain gold should be close to the rate here; the price of a finished piece will always be higher.

What is the difference between 24K, 22K and 21K gold?

Karat measures purity: 24K is pure gold (99.9%), 22K is 22 parts gold out of 24 (91.6% pure), and 21K is 87.5% pure. 22K is the standard for jewellery in Pakistan because pure gold is too soft for everyday wear. The price scales with purity — a tola of 22K gold is worth 22/24 of a tola of 24K gold.

How many grams are in one tola?

One tola is 11.6638 grams, or 0.375 of a troy ounce. One troy ounce — the unit the world gold market uses — is 31.1035 grams, or about 2.67 tolas. The gold converter on this site converts between tolas, grams, 10 grams and troy ounces at the live rate.

What is the difference between the interbank and open market dollar rate?

The interbank rate is the rate banks use when trading with each other, and it is the benchmark the State Bank of Pakistan publishes and the news quotes — it is the rate shown on this site. The open market rate is what exchange companies and money changers offer the public for cash, and it includes a spread: you buy dollars slightly above the interbank rate and sell them slightly below it.

Why does the dollar rate change every day?

The rate is set by supply and demand for dollars in the interbank market. Pakistan earns dollars through exports, remittances and investment, and spends them on imports, debt repayments and travel. When outflows exceed inflows, the rupee weakens; when inflows exceed outflows, it strengthens. The US Federal Reserve's interest rate decisions also move the dollar against every currency, including the rupee.

Is gold a good investment?

Gold is a store of value and a hedge against rupee depreciation, not a growth investment: it pays no income while you hold it, and its price can fall for years. Bars and coins are the cheapest way to own it as an investment; jewellery carries making charges that are lost on resale. Most advisers suggest a modest share of a portfolio — often quoted as 5 to 10%. This site is educational only, not financial advice.

Where do your rates come from?

Gold prices come from a live international spot-price feed, and currency rates come from a live foreign-exchange feed. The sources are named on each rate page. If a feed is unavailable, the page says so plainly instead of showing a stored or estimated figure.

Do you store the numbers I type into the converters?

No. Every calculation on this site runs entirely in your own browser. Nothing you type is sent to us or to any third party, nothing is stored, and it disappears when you close the page. See the Privacy Policy for the full details.
