How the conversion works

Currency conversion here runs in two steps through the US dollar, which is how the world's currency markets quote most pairs. Every rate on this page is fetched live against the dollar, and converting between any two currencies — say euros to rupees — is done by converting euros to dollars first, then dollars to rupees. The maths is exact for the rates shown; the only reason a bank or money changer would give you a different figure is their spread and fees.

The gold converter works from the live spot price per troy ounce. Your weight is converted to grams (one tola is 11.6638 grams, one troy ounce is 31.1035 grams), multiplied by the live price per gram for the purity you chose. Remember that a jeweller buying your gold will pay the metal value, not the making charges you paid when you bought the piece.
