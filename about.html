About Gold & Dollar Rates Pakistan

A small independent site that answers two questions Pakistanis ask every day.

Gold & Dollar Rates Pakistan is a small independent website that answers two questions Pakistanis ask every day: what is the price of gold today, and what is the dollar worth in rupees? We publish live rates, converters and plain-language explainers — nothing more, and nothing less.

How the rates work

Gold prices are calculated from the live international spot price of gold per troy ounce, converted at the live US dollar to Pakistani rupee rate. One tola is 11.6638 grams (0.375 of a troy ounce); 22K and 21K prices are the 24K price scaled by purity. Currency rates come from a live foreign-exchange feed based on the interbank market. Every rate is fetched fresh when you load the page — we never store a rate, never estimate one, and if a data feed is down the page says so instead of showing a stale number.

What this site is not

We are not a bank, a jeweller, an exchange company or a financial adviser. We do not buy or sell gold or currency, we do not take deposits, and nothing on this site is financial advice. The rates are indicative: the price a jeweller or money changer quotes you will differ, because they add their own charges and spreads. Use our figures as a guide, and confirm the final price before any transaction.

Why we built it

Rate websites too often show yesterday's numbers, bury the rate under pop-ups, or mix advertising with the figures. We wanted a fast, clean page that shows today's rate first, explains how it is calculated, and respects the reader's time. The guides and articles are written by us, in plain language, for buyers, savers and families sending money home.

Contact

Spotted a wrong number, or want a currency or calculator we do not have yet? Use the contact page — every message is read.
